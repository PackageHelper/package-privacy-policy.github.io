1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MedConnect SOFTWARE ("MedConnect", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the MedConnect SOFTWARE Android application ("App").

MedConnect SOFTWARE is an application distribution platform intended for authorized users. The App allows authorized users to access applications made available through the platform, download application packages, receive notifications, and use related application-management functionality.

By using MedConnect SOFTWARE, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, MedConnect may process the following categories of information.

2.1 Authentication and Access Information

MedConnect uses an authentication mechanism to control access to the application platform. Information associated with authentication may include an access code or authentication credentials and authentication/session information such as a security token.

Authentication information is used to verify that a user is authorized to access MedConnect and to maintain an authenticated session.

2.2 Push Notification Information

If push notifications are enabled, MedConnect may collect and store a device's push notification token, such as an Expo push token, so that the MedConnect backend can send notifications to the appropriate device.

Push notification information may be associated with the authorized user's account or device information where required for notification delivery.

Push tokens are used only for notification delivery and related notification management.

2.3 Device and Technical Information

The App and the services it uses may process limited technical information necessary to provide, secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the service.

This may include information such as application version, operating-system-related information, device-related identifiers, push notification tokens, network information, and technical error information where available.

2.4 Application and Download Information

MedConnect may process information related to applications made available through the platform, including application names, versions, package information, download requests, and installation-related actions.

APK files may be downloaded from servers or storage locations used by the MedConnect platform. Installation of an APK is initiated by the user through the Android operating system.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through MedConnect may be used for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate authorized users.
  • To provide access to the MedConnect application platform.
  • To maintain authenticated sessions.
  • To deliver push notifications.
  • To provide application downloads.
  • To provide application version and update information.
  • To maintain and operate the MedConnect service.
  • To detect and troubleshoot technical problems.
  • To protect the service against unauthorized access or misuse.
  • To maintain service security and reliability.

4. Push Notifications

MedConnect may send push notifications to devices that have registered for notifications.

Notifications may include service announcements, application updates, reminders, administrative messages, or other information related to the MedConnect service.

You can control notification permissions through the Android operating system settings. Disabling notifications may prevent you from receiving certain MedConnect notifications.

5. Third-Party Services

MedConnect may use third-party infrastructure and services to provide specific functionality.

5.1 Expo Push Notification Service

MedConnect may use Expo's push notification infrastructure to facilitate delivery of push notifications.

Expo may process information necessary to provide its notification services in accordance with its own privacy practices.

5.2 Firebase / Google Services

Android push notification delivery may use Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) and related Google services.

These services may process technical information required for notification delivery according to Google's applicable privacy policies.

For more information, please review Google's privacy information at https://policies.google.com/privacy .

6. Data Storage and Security

MedConnect stores certain information on backend systems used to operate the service. Security measures are implemented to help protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Authentication and administrative functionality is protected through server-side authorization mechanisms.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, maintain, secure, and operate the MedConnect service, or as required for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Push notification tokens may be removed or deactivated when they are no longer valid or when they are no longer required for notification delivery.

8. Data Sharing

MedConnect does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the MedConnect platform, such as hosting, database, push notification, and infrastructure providers.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal processes, or valid government requests.

9. APK Downloads and Installation

MedConnect may provide Android application packages (APK files) for authorized users to download.

The user controls whether an application package is installed. Installation is initiated through the Android operating system and requires the user's action.

MedConnect does not silently install applications without user interaction.

10. Permissions

MedConnect may request Android permissions required for its functionality.

These permissions may include permissions required to access network services, receive notifications, and facilitate application-package downloads and installation.

Permissions are used only for functionality supported by the application.

11. User Choices

Depending on the functionality available in your version of the App, you may control certain permissions through Android device settings.

You may disable push notifications through your Android notification settings. Disabling notifications does not necessarily remove your authorization to use the MedConnect platform.

12. Children's Privacy

MedConnect SOFTWARE is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through MedConnect in circumstances where such collection is not permitted, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and handled appropriately.
